Before You Start

Everything You'd Want to Know Before You Begin.

From choosing the right approach to understanding the consultation, treatment and pricing — here are the answers to the questions we hear most often.

Medically reviewed by Dr. Shah Nawaz, MD Medicine

01What does Glossary offer?

Glossary offers two main approaches: doctor-guided GLP-1 weight management and targeted body-contouring treatments for the abdomen, hips and thighs.

Your approach is personalised around your goals. Depending on your needs and suitability, your journey may include medical guidance, nutrition and lifestyle support, ongoing monitoring, or targeted body-contouring services.

You don't need to know exactly which option is right before you enquire.

02I'm not sure whether I need GLP-1 or body contouring. What should I do?

That's completely fine — you don't have to choose on your own.

Start with a consultation and tell us what you're trying to achieve. The team can understand your goals and discuss which approach may be appropriate for you.

Your goal comes first; the treatment comes after.

03How does the GLP-1 consultation work?

The process starts with a medical consultation where you can discuss your goals, health history and expectations with a qualified medical expert.

Your suitability for a GLP-1-based approach is assessed before any prescription-based protocol is started. If appropriate, your journey can include structured support, nutrition and lifestyle guidance, and ongoing monitoring.

The first step is simply understanding whether it's right for you.

04Is GLP-1 suitable for everyone?

No. Suitability needs to be assessed individually.

A medical professional will consider your circumstances, goals and relevant health information during the consultation before determining whether a GLP-1-based approach is appropriate.

That's why Glossary starts with medical assessment rather than simply recommending a medication.

05What happens during an inch-loss treatment?

Our abdomen, hips and thighs treatments use a multi-step, guided approach. The treatment currently includes:

  • G5 massage
  • Ultrasonic cavitation
  • Radio-frequency (RF)
  • Manual massage / lymphatic drainage
  • Heat therapy + EMS

The exact treatment focus depends on the area being addressed. You can discuss your goals with the team before beginning.

06How long are the abdomen, hips and thighs treatments?

The current treatment durations are:

  • Abdomen: 90 minutes
  • Hips: 120 minutes
  • Thighs: 120 minutes

Each is a guided, in-studio session focused on the selected area. You can explore the treatment specific to your area before booking.

07How much do the inch-loss treatments cost?

The current starting price is from ₹4,050.

This applies to the listed abdomen, hips and thighs inch-loss services, with the treatment duration varying by area.

If you're unsure which treatment is appropriate, you can start with a consultation and discuss your goals before deciding.

08How many inch-loss sessions will I need?

There isn't one fixed number that applies to everyone.

The appropriate approach depends on your individual goals and how your body responds to the treatment. For the hips service, the current recommended schedule is one session every 7–10 days, with 6–8 sessions suggested for visible inch-loss, followed by maintenance as appropriate.

Your treatment plan should be discussed with the Glossary team rather than assuming the same schedule applies to everyone.

09Can I choose just one area?

Yes. The body-contouring services are area-specific. You can explore treatments focused on:

  • Abdomen
  • Hips
  • Thighs

This is particularly relevant if your concern is a specific area rather than overall weight management. If you're unsure which area or approach is right for you, start with a consultation.

10What happens after I enquire?

We'll start by understanding what you're looking to achieve. You'll be able to share whether you're interested in:

  • GLP-1 weight management
  • Abdomen inch loss
  • Hips inch loss
  • Thighs inch loss
  • Or you're simply not sure where to start

From there, the Glossary team can guide you toward the appropriate next step. You don't need to have everything figured out before you contact us.

11Will I be pressured to purchase a treatment?

No. Every enquiry starts with a conversation to understand your goals — there's no obligation to proceed with any treatment.

GLP-1 suitability is assessed by a medical professional before any prescription-based protocol is discussed, and body-contouring options are explained clearly so you can decide what's right for you, at your own pace.

12Who provides the treatment? Who are the doctors?

Glossary's programs are doctor-guided. GLP-1 consultations and eligibility assessments are conducted by qualified medical professionals, and body-contouring treatments are delivered by a trained team under medical supervision.

Specific practitioner details are shared during your consultation.
