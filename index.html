FAQs

Common Questions, Answered

What is a GLP-1 weight management program?

GLP-1 programs use a prescription-based weekly clinical protocol, prescribed and adjusted by a doctor, alongside nutrition and lifestyle support to help manage appetite and metabolism as part of a structured weight-management plan.

How does the consultation work?

You'll have a virtual consultation with an endocrinologist or medical expert who reviews your goals and medical history before any clinical protocol is personalised for you.

Do I need medical screening before starting?

Yes. Every program begins with a medical consultation and baseline diagnostics to confirm the program is clinically appropriate for you before anything is prescribed.

Is the program suitable for everyone?

No — these programs are generally intended for adults with a BMI of 30+, or 27+ with related conditions such as hypertension, pre-diabetes or PCOS. They are not suitable for minors, those who are pregnant or planning pregnancy, or people with certain medical conditions. Your doctor confirms suitability during consultation.

Are there side effects?

As with any prescription-based clinical protocol, some clients experience temporary digestive side effects. This is why programs include a GI-relief kit and doctor-led dose titration, with your care team monitoring your response throughout.

How long is the program?

Core programs typically run over a 90-day (12-week) period with weekly protocol support and monthly medical reviews, with optional maintenance available afterwards.

Do I need to visit a clinic?

No — consultations, monitoring and coaching are delivered virtually and at home. In-person sessions (such as body-contouring or slimming sessions, where included) are also conducted at your home.

Is the treatment delivered at home?

Yes. Glossary Skin's model is built around at-home delivery across Delhi, Noida and Gurugram.

Which program is right for me?

It depends on your goals, medical history and whether you're also looking for body contouring support. Your doctor will assess your suitability during the consultation and recommend the right fit — select "Not sure — help me choose" in the booking form and our team will guide you.

What happens after I submit the form?

You'll receive an immediate confirmation with your selected program, date and time. Our team will follow up by phone to confirm your consultation slot.

What happens after the initial consultation?

If the program is clinically appropriate for you, your doctor personalises your protocol and you begin your weekly plan, with nutrition support and monthly progress reviews.

What if I am not sure which program to choose?

That's completely fine — select "Not sure — help me choose" and our team will help you evaluate your options before your consultation.

What happens after the initial program period?

SemaTone 360 and WegoTone 360 include optional maintenance after the 90-day core program for continued support or tapering. Your doctor will discuss next steps based on your progress.
